About Me

Hello! I’m Jane, a passionate photographer based in Seattle. I love exploring the world through my lens, capturing the raw emotions of people and the serene beauty of nature. My work blends storytelling with visual artistry, aiming to evoke feelings and spark curiosity.

When I’m not behind the camera, you’ll find me hiking, sipping coffee, or curating my ever-growing collection of vintage lenses.
